WHY IT MATTERS

Steam curing should not depend on memory and guesswork.

In a busy precast factory, manual steam control can make curing dependent on shift experience, timing and frequent checks. SALVENR gives the operator a defined process and gives QA a record of what actually happened.

USE CASES

One control concept. Multiple precast processes.

Configure the cure around the product and production method instead of forcing every product into the same cycle.

01

Jacking & large-diameter pipe

Track concrete temperature through long steam cures and retain a traceable record for each production cycle.

02

Box culverts & heavy precast

Apply controlled ramp rates to reduce thermal shock while maintaining production-focused cure times.

03

Dedicated curing chambers

Give operators a clear chamber status with target, actual temperature, progress and steam demand visible at once.

04

Match curing · Professional

Planned advanced architecture can reproduce the product thermal profile in a separate test-cylinder curing bath.
